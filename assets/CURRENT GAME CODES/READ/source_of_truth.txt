## 1. Core Gameplay Mechanics

### Investigation Phases & Timers
- **Setup Phase**: The first **120 seconds** (2 minutes) of a match. No hunts can occur during this time.
- **Hunt Cooldown**: After a hunt ends, there is a **25-second** cooldown before another can begin.
- **Smudge Stick Prevention**: Using a Smudge Stick near the ghost prevents a hunt for **90 seconds**. This is extended to **180 seconds** for a Spirit.

### Hiding & Line of Sight (LoS)
- During a hunt, the ghost actively searches for the player.
- Breaking line of sight by hiding behind furniture or closing doors is the primary survival mechanic.
- Some ghosts (e.g., Revenant) have abilities directly tied to whether they can see the player. LoS is determined by a direct raycast from the ghost to the player.

### Player Stats
- **Health**: The player has no health bar. Being caught by the ghost during a hunt results in instant death.
- **Sanity**: Starts at 100%. This is the player's primary resource, and its depletion is the main trigger for hunts.
- **Movement Speed**:
    - **Walking**: 3.5 m/s
    - **Sprinting**: 5.6 m/s (1.6x multiplier)
    - **Crouching**: 1.5 m/s
- **Stamina**: Used for sprinting.
    - Drains over 10 seconds of continuous sprinting.
    - Fully recharges over 20 seconds.

### Sanity System
Sanity drain is calculated periodically.
- **Drain Triggers**:
    - **Darkness**: Being in a dark room is the primary cause of sanity drain. Being in a lit area prevents it entirely.
    - **Ghost Proximity**: Being near the ghost increases sanity drain.
- **Ghost-Specific Effects**:
    - **Yurei**: Has a stronger passive sanity drain effect on players.
    - **Phantom**: Looking at a Phantom causes a significant sanity drop.
    - **Moroi**: Curses a player via Spirit Box, doubling their sanity drain in the dark.
- **Sanity Restoration**:
    - **Sanity Medication**: Instantly restores **40% sanity**.

### Temperature System
- The house temperature is a key clue for finding the ghost's location.
- **Breaker ON**: The target temperature for most rooms is **20°C**.
- **Breaker OFF**: Rooms will slowly cool to the map's **ambient temperature**.
- **Ghost Favorite Room**: This room's target temperature is **-10°C**. It will eventually cause Freezing Temperatures evidence (< 0°C).
- **Ghost Presence**: The room the ghost is currently in will be chilled by an additional **-3°C**.
- **Ambient Temperatures** (Breaker OFF target):
    - **Calm / Fog**: 13°C
    - **Rain**: 8°C
    - **Snow**: 5°C

### Weather Effects
- **Calm**: No special effects.
- **Rain / Snow**: Affects ambient temperature.
- **Fog**: Adds visual fog to the environment.
- **Blood Moon**:
    - Sanity drain is **doubled**.
    - All ghost hunt speeds are increased by **15%**.

## 2. Evidence Types

- **EMF Level 5**: Read on the EMF Reader.
- **Spirit Box**: A vocal response through the Spirit Box.
- **Fingerprints**: Visible with the UV Light on doors, light switches.
- **Ghost Orb**: A floating orb, visible only through a video camera feed.
- **Ghost Writing**: The ghost writes in the Ghost Writing Book.
- **Freezing Temperatures**: Visible breath and a thermometer reading of < 0°C.
- **D.O.T.S. Projector**: A silhouette of the ghost running through the green laser grid.

## 3. Equipment Details

- **Flashlight**: Primary light source.
- **UV Light**: Reveals Fingerprints and ghost footprints.
- **EMF Reader**: Detects paranormal interactions. EMF 5 is evidence.
- **Spirit Box**: Responds only if the ghost has Spirit Box as evidence.
- **Photo Camera**: **5 uses**.
- **Video Camera**: Used to find Ghost Orbs. Features IR mode.
- **Tripod**: A stand for a video camera.
- **Ghost Writing Book**: Placeable surface for the ghost to write in.
- **D.O.T.S. Projector**: Projects a laser grid to reveal ghost silhouettes.
- **Thermometer**: Measures room temperature.
- **Smudge Sticks**: **1 use**. Must be lit with Lighter. Deters hunts.
- **Lighter**: Slotless item. Used to light Smudge Sticks.
- **Crucifix**: **2 uses**. Prevents a hunt if placed within **3 meters** of the ghost's spawn point (**5 meters** for a Demon).
- **Salt**: **3 uses**. Wraiths cannot step in it. Other ghosts leave footprints visible with a UV Light.
- **Sanity Medication**: **1 use**. Restores 40% sanity.
- **Lantern**: Placeable light source that prevents sanity drain in a radius.
- **Parabolic Microphone**: Detects faint paranormal sounds from a distance.
